# COMSAI Student User Manual

COMSAI is the College of Computer Studies (CCS) student information assistant. Students can use the chat without creating an account or signing in.

## Open COMSAI

Open the student page provided by your school. For a local XAMPP installation, it is `http://localhost/COMSAI%20SYSTEM/`. On a deployed site, use the site's home page.

## Ask a question

1. Type a question in the box at the bottom of the chat and select **Send**, or press **Enter**.
2. Press **Shift+Enter** to add a new line without sending.
3. You can also select a suggested question or a topic in **Information topics** to ask about that area.
4. Start a separate conversation with **New chat**.

Questions can be up to 1,000 characters. Ask one clear question at a time and include enough detail to identify the CCS topic.

## Understand answers

COMSAI answers CCS-specific questions using information approved for the knowledge base and published announcements. Where available, source titles or links appear below the answer; use them to check details with the official source.

The answer label indicates the kind of response:

- **Knowledge base answer** or **AI answer - based on CCS sources**: based on matching CCS information.
- **Published announcement**: based on a current published notice.
- **No verified CCS match**: COMSAI could not find a verified answer. Check an official CCS channel or contact the appropriate office rather than relying on an unverified answer.
- **General AI - not verified CCS information**: a general response, not official CCS guidance.

General replies may be AI-generated. Even when sources are shown, confirm time-sensitive or important details with the cited source or an official CCS channel.

## Send feedback

Open **Send student feedback** in the side panel, enter up to 3,000 characters, and select **Submit feedback**. A counter shows how many characters you have entered. Feedback is submitted anonymously and can be reviewed by administrators. Do not include your name, student number, contact details, passwords, or other personal or sensitive information.

## If COMSAI is unavailable

If the page says **Under maintenance**, chat is temporarily disabled; try again later. If the daily usage limit is reached, come back the next day. For a connection or server error, refresh the page and retry later. Contact your school's support channel if the issue persists.

## Privacy and responsible use

- You do not need a student account to chat. The application associates conversations with a random anonymous session token, not a student profile.
- Chat questions and answers may be stored for service operation and can be reviewed by authorized administrators. Avoid entering personal, confidential, or sensitive information.
- Feedback is also visible to authorized administrators, so keep it appropriate and non-sensitive.
- Do not use COMSAI as a substitute for official school instructions, emergency assistance, or professional advice.
